Google Play Store Data Safety & Deletion Policy

DawaLens AI • Account & Data Deletion Portal

In full compliance with the Google Play Store Account Deletion Policy, users have the absolute right to permanently delete their DawaLens AI account, stored medication entries, history logs, and profile records.

What Data Is Permanently Deleted?
  • Google Account Credentials & Profile: Email address, user ID, display name, and authentication tokens.
  • Medication Vault: All recorded medicines, active dosages, batch numbers, remaining quantities, and schedule notes.
  • Audit History: All chronological logs of doses taken, edits, and timestamps.
  • On-Device Photos: Local device camera image caches stored in IndexedDB browser memory are deleted.

Retention Period: None. All records are permanently erased within 48 hours of request receipt (or immediately if executed in-app).

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

  1. In-App Deletion (Instant): Sign in to your account, open the Profile Menu, click Delete Account & Data, and confirm deletion. All data is deleted immediately.

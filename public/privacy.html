This Privacy Policy details our standards regarding the collection, processing, storage, encryption, sharing, and permanent deletion of user data in full adherence to the Google API Services User Data Policy (including the Limited Use requirements) and the Google Play Store Data Safety Policy.

1. Google API Services User Data & Limited Use Disclosure

Mandatory Compliance Disclosure:
DawaLens AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

DawaLens AI provides users with the option to sign in using Google Sign-In. When you authenticate using Google, we access and process your information strictly within the following boundaries:

  • OAuth Scopes Requested: We request only fundamental identity scopes: openid, profile (name, profile picture), and email. We do NOT request access to your Google Drive files, Gmail inboxes, Google Contacts, Google Photos, or any external Google Workspace services.
  • User-Facing Features Only: We only access, use, and process your Google user data to provide prominent user-facing features within the DawaLens AI interface (such as authenticating your account, securing your medicine database, and dispatching your requested medication expiry reminder emails).
  • No Transfer Except for Service Provision: We do not transfer or disclose your Google user data to third parties unless strictly necessary to provide or improve the user-facing features of DawaLens AI, to comply with applicable laws, or as part of a formal corporate acquisition or merger with prior notice.
  • No Advertising or Marketing Resale: We do NOT use or transfer Google user data to serve advertisements, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising. We never sell your Google data to third parties, data brokers, or marketing networks.
  • Strict Prohibition on AI Model Training: We do NOT use, share, or transfer Google user data, user profile information, or medication entries to train, re-train, fine-tune, or ground generic artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), or Large Language Models (LLMs).
  • Human Access Restrictions: No human personnel at DawaLens AI is permitted to read your personal Google data unless: (1) we have obtained your explicit affirmative consent for a specific technical support request, (2) it is required for security investigations (such as debugging fraud or abuse), (3) it is necessary to comply with applicable law, or (4) the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal system health operations.

2. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

We collect information in three ways: directly from your input, through device permissions you affirmatively grant, and automatically through system diagnostics:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Identifiers: Your full name, email address, profile picture URL, and Firebase Auth UID obtained via Google Sign-In or email registration.
  • Medication Vault Data: Medicine brand and generic names, dosage strengths (e.g., 500mg, 10ml), dosage form (tablets, capsules, syrup, inhaler, drops, injection), expiration dates, batch numbers, remaining quantities, dosage schedules (morning, afternoon, evening, night), administration instructions (before or after food), and custom tags.
  • Health & Consultation Inquiries: Any questions, symptoms, or medication names you type or speak into the AI Pharmacist consultation chat.
  • Audit & Intake Logs: Timestamps when you record taking a scheduled dose or updating your medicine supply.

B. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

DawaLens AI requests specific device permissions strictly to perform on-device operations:

Permission Purpose Data Handling Standard
Camera (CAMERA) Allows scanning medicine boxes, blister packs, and prescription labels for text recognition (OCR) and visual packaging classification. Hybrid Processing & Local Vault: Medicine photos in your vault are stored 100% locally in sandboxed IndexedDB storage and are NEVER saved to Firebase Storage or cloud databases. During online scanning, photos are streamed over TLS 1.3 encrypted HTTPS to Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for multimodal OCR (zero PII attached, ephemeral memory processing, zero disk caching). In offline mode, OCR runs 100% on-device via Tesseract.js.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Delivers scheduled medication alarms and reminders for medicines nearing their expiration date (at 30 days, 7 days, and on the expiration day). Notifications are generated locally on your device or via secure Firebase Cloud Messaging. No marketing or promotional push notifications are ever sent.

C. Information Collected Automatically

  • Diagnostics & Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable telemetry (such as session duration, feature usage, browser type, device brand, and crash reports). IP addresses are automatically anonymized. We strictly forbid transmitting medication names, health records, or authentication tokens to analytics services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  1. Identity Verification: To authenticate your sign-in session and protect your account from unauthorized access.
  2. Medicine Inventory Synchronization: To synchronize your medicine inventory across your authenticated mobile, desktop, and web clients via Firebase Firestore.
  3. Safety & Expiry Reminders: To automatically calculate expiration timelines and route timely email alerts to your verified email address via transactional email infrastructure.
  4. Drug Interaction Checking: To screen your entered medications for known contraindications and drug-drug interactions using clinical rules and the Google Gemini API.
  5. Customer Support: To resolve technical issues or assist you with account deletion inquiries.

4. Camera, Prescription Photo & Image Architecture

Hybrid Processing & Sandboxed Local Storage: Medicine packaging photographs saved into your inventory are stored 100% locally on your physical device in sandboxed IndexedDB storage (DawaLensLocalImages) and are never uploaded to Firebase Storage, S3, or remote databases. During online scanning, photos are streamed over secure TLS 1.3 encrypted HTTPS to Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for multimodal OCR (zero PII attached, ephemeral memory processing, zero disk caching). In offline mode, OCR runs 100% locally via client-side Tesseract.js and on-device CNN. Deleting a medication entry or deleting your account permanently purges the local image from your device.

5. AI Pharmacist & Large Language Model (LLM) Processing

DawaLens AI features an interactive AI Pharmacist companion ("Dr. DawaLens") powered by a hybrid architecture of Google Gemini API and an on-device Small Language Model (SLM):

  • No Personal Identifiers Sent to AI: When you ask a medical or medication question, only the text of your clinical query and your relevant medicine list are passed to the AI model. Your name, email address, Google account ID, and authentication tokens are never passed to the Gemini API.
  • No Model Training: Your medical inquiries and medicine vault data are not used to train or improve Google's foundation AI models.
  • Offline Fallback: If you are offline or if the remote API is unavailable, the on-device SLM model answers your queries entirely within your local browser sandbox with zero network transmission.

7. Data Security and Encryption

We maintain strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) with HSTS enabled.
  • Encryption at Rest: All database records stored in Google Cloud Firestore are encrypted using enterprise-grade AES-256 encryption.
  • Access Controls: Firebase Security Rules strictly isolate database access so that each user can only read and write their own private medication records.
  • Zero Plaintext Credentials: We never handle or store passwords for Google accounts. Authentication is handled entirely through Google's secure OAuth 2.0 flow.

8. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

In accordance with the Google Play Store Data Safety and Account Deletion Policy, users have full autonomy over their personal records:

  • Retention Schedule: We retain your personal data and medication vault entries only for as long as your account remains active.
  • How to Delete Your Account and Data:
    1. In-App Self-Service (Instant): Sign in to your account, open the Profile Menu, navigate to Account Settings, click "Delete Account & Data", and confirm. All Firestore database documents, audit history, and authentication records are permanently erased immediately.
    2. Web Deletion Portal (Without App): You can delete your account at any time without reinstalling the app by visiting our dedicated Account Deletion Page (https://dawalens.vercel.app/delete-account).
    3. Revoke Google OAuth Access: You can disconnect DawaLens AI's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Security - Third-party apps.
    4. Email Deletion Request: You may also email our data protection team directly at mdnoor4860@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Email deletion requests are verified and completed within 48 to 72 hours.

9. Your Legal Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request a complete summary of all personal and medication data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit or correct any inaccurate medication entries, dosages, or profile information directly in the app.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the absolute right to have all your personal and health records permanently deleted.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can export your complete medication inventory into a CSV spreadsheet at any time for transfer to Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel, or your medical provider.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke permissions (Camera, Notifications, or Google OAuth authorization) at any time through your device settings or Google security portal.

If we discover that an individual under the age of 13 has created an account without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete that account and all associated records from our servers.

Google OAuth & Play Store Data Safety

Privacy Policy for DawaLens AI

Effective Date: June 25, 2026

DawaLens AI ("we", "our", or "the Service") operates the digital pharmacy assistant application available at https://noorpos.in. This Privacy Policy details our standards regarding the collection, handling, encryption, and deletion of user data in full adherence to the Google API Services User Data Policy (including Limited Use requirements) and the Google Play Store Data Safety Policy.

1. Google User Data Handling (Limited Use)

A. What Google Data We Access

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In, we securely retrieve only your primary Google account identifier: email address, display name, and profile avatar URL. We do NOT request access to contacts, Google Drive, Gmail messages, or private external files.

B. How We Use Your Google Data

  • To securely authenticate your session and associate your medication inventory with your account.
  • To route automated expiry alerts and low-stock reminder emails to your designated email address.
  • No Model Training: None of your Google user data, profile info, or medication entries are ever used to train, fine-tune, or ground generic AI, machine learning models, or large language models.

C. Data Sharing

We do not share or sell your Google user data. Your information is never sold, traded, or transferred to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

2. App-Specific Medicine & Photo Privacy

100% On-Device Image Storage: Any images or prescription photos scanned using your browser's camera are stored entirely on-device (within secure local IndexedDB storage) and are never transmitted to our servers or processed off-device.

If you delete a medication log, the corresponding local image is permanently destroyed from your device memory.

3. Google Analytics Disclosure

We use Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated, anonymous diagnostic usage statistics to optimize app performance. We strictly forbid transmitting sensitive personal health information, encryption keys, or medication photos to Google Analytics.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

In accordance with the Google Play Store Account Deletion Policy, users can permanently delete their account and all associated cloud data at any time via the in-app menu or our Account Deletion Page. Deletions are processed immediately (or within 48 hours for email requests).

5. Compliance and Contact

For any privacy queries or data subject requests, email us directly at: dawalens@noorpos.in

Google Play Store & OAuth Data Deletion Portal

DawaLens AI • Account & Data Deletion Portal

Application: DawaLens AI • Domain: https://dawalens.vercel.app

In full compliance with the Google Play Store Account Deletion Policy and Google API Services User Data Policy, all users have the absolute right to permanently delete their DawaLens AI account, stored medication entries, dose logs, and personal profile records.

What Data Is Permanently Purged?
  • Authentication & Profile Identifiers: Google account ID, email address, display name, profile photo, and Firebase Auth UID.
  • Medication Vault: All recorded medicines, dosages, forms, batch numbers, remaining quantities, and schedule timings stored in Firestore.
  • Dose Audit History: Chronological records of completed doses and notifications.
  • On-Device Photo Cache: Sandboxed IndexedDB browser memory containing scanned packaging images is wiped.

Retention Timeline: Zero. In-app deletions take effect immediately. Email requests are verified and permanently purged within 48 to 72 hours.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

  1. In-App Deletion (Instant):

    Open the DawaLens AI application → Click your Profile Avatar in the top header → Select Account Settings → Click the red "Delete Account & Data" button → Confirm deletion. Your entire account, cloud database documents, and local caches are instantly and irreversibly destroyed.

  2. Web Deletion Request (Without App):

    If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, send an email request using the button below:

    Request Account Deletion via Email
  3. Revoke Google Account Authorization:

    You can revoke DawaLens AI's access to your Google credentials at any time directly through Google's security settings at: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

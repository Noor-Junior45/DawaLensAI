DawaLens AI • User Guide & Operating Manual

Welcome to DawaLens AI, your personal digital pharmacy and medication manager. This manual outlines all features, scanning procedures, and account settings.

1. Adding Medications & AI Camera Vision

You can add medications to your digital medicine cabinet via two modes:

  • AI Prescription & Label Scanner: Tap the Scan button to capture any medication box or prescription label. On-device vision extracts drug name, strength dosage, and expiration dates.
  • Manual Smart Input: Type a medication name to get auto-fill suggestions. Specify dosage form, remaining pill count, and expiry date.

2. Expiration Batch Tracking

DawaLens AI groups multiple purchases of the same medication into a single unified card. It displays the nearest expiring batch first (FIFO) so you use earlier batches before newer ones.

3. Drug Interaction Checks

When you have 2 or more active medications in your inventory, use the Check Interactions tool in the Assistant tab to scan for contraindications, food warnings, or duplicate therapies.

Clinical Reference Disclaimer: AI-generated interaction warnings and summaries are for educational reference only. Always consult a licensed doctor or pharmacist before modifying therapies.

4. Email & Notification Alerts

Enable automated email reminders in your Profile settings. Receive weekly alerts for medications expiring within 30, 60, or 90 days directly in your inbox.

5. Data Privacy & Account Deletion

Photos captured by your camera are stored locally on your physical device (IndexedDB) and are never uploaded to remote servers. You can request permanent account and data deletion at any time via our Account Deletion Page.
